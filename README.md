# CareerGhana: Research Findings

This repository contains my research findings and deliverables completed during my remote internship. The research focuses on the intersection of EdTech, career platforms, and learning outcomes in Ghana and across Africa.

## 📂 Repository Contents

### 1. Task 1: Literature Review (EdTech & Learning Outcomes)
A systematic review of recent literature on Technology-Enhanced Learning (TEL) in Africa, analyzing its impact on learning outcomes, the usage gap, and implementation challenges.
- **File:** `TASK_1.pdf`

### 2. Task 2: EdTech & Career Platform Landscape
An analysis of three major platforms (YouthXplore, Jobberman Ghana, ALX) operating in Ghana and Africa, identifying systemic gaps in the career landscape and proposing opportunities.
- **File:** `TASK_2.pdf`

### 3. Task 5: Research-Backed Brief (The Future of EdTech)
A research brief exploring the potential of AI tutoring via basic mobile phones in Ghana as a low-cost path to learning gains.
- **File:** `TASK_5.pdf`

## 🛠 Methodology & Tools
- **Research:** Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus, IEEE Xplore.
- **Formatting:** LaTeX (source code included in respective folders).
